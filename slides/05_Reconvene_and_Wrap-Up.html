Slide 5: What you practiced today

  • Identify which NaNDA datasets fit your research question, by geographic level, years covered, and topic
  • Link NaNDA to your own data using geographic identifiers, including when to use tract_fips10 or tract_fips20, and how ZIP codes differ from ZCTAs
  • Frame a research question that combines neighborhood measures with your individual-level aging outcomes

Slide 7: Cite the dataset you use

  • Every NaNDA dataset has its own DOI. Include it in your citation.
  • Cite each dataset you used, rather than NaNDA as a whole.
  • Copy the ready-made citation from the dataset's page on ICPSR.
  • More on citing in the NaNDA FAQs
