# Breakout 2 (intermediate): Linking NaNDA with Your Data

Intermediate session, 45 minutes. Presenters: Robert Melendez (he|him), Data Analyst; Will Clary (he|him), Research Data Analyst; and Grace Noppert (she|her), Research Associate Professor and NaNDA Co-Principal Investigator. All three are at the University of Michigan, Institute for Social Research.

Pick this session if your data have addresses, ZIP codes, or census tracts to merge with NaNDA. You'll leave with a merge you ran on practice data, plus code you can reuse.

## What the materials are

The materials for this session are published separately and linked from this page. They are a walkthrough page that shows every step with its output in R, with the same step in Stata and Python one tab away; a materials ZIP with synthetic practice data, a ZIP-to-ZCTA crosswalk, and the walkthrough source; and NaNDA files, which come from the [NaNDA repository on ICPSR](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/sites/nanda) with a free account.
